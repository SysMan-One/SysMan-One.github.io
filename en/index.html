Free software for those who value small, clear and verifiable things.

A DEC Alpha emulator that boots OpenVMS, a proxy server for small Linux devices, a MODBUS gateway and an ecosystem of utilities around them: networking, backups, editors, libraries. The source is open, the documentation is in Russian and English.
