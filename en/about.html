About

Who we are

The projects are released by StarLet Squad and Ruslan R. Laishev (AKA: BadAss SysMan); mbus-gw-t2r together with the NaPi Lab team. The code is written in the OpenVMS style: modules with a revision history, version idents like X01-nnECOmm, messages from a catalogue of condition values, help in the HELP format.

How to report a bug

Open an Issue in the project's repository (the links are on the project's page). Give the version (-v or version), the platform and a piece of the log without passwords or keys.

A channel for questions outside the repositories (mail, chat) is not decided yet.
